💡 Quick Tips

Reading: Skim the passage first (2-3 min), then read questions carefully. Watch for paraphrasing — answers rarely use the exact same words as the passage.
Listening: Use the preview time to read questions before the audio plays. Listen for signpost words like "however," "actually," and "the main point is."
Timing: Spend no more than 20 minutes per reading passage. In listening, you have 10 minutes to transfer answers at the end.
Strategy: Don't leave any answers blank — there's no penalty for wrong answers. Make an educated guess if you're unsure.
